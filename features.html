What Made It Special

Six Devices, One Pocket

Before "smartphone" was even a common word, the Treo 650 was already living up to the idea — folding a phone, a PDA, a keyboard, a camera and an internet connection into a single handset. Here's a closer look at each piece.

📧
Communication

Email Anywhere

The Treo 650 let people read and reply to email without being tied to a desk — a genuinely new idea for most owners in 2004.

  • Push-style syncing with popular email providers
  • Threaded inbox built for the small screen
  • Attachments viewable through the Documents app
⌨️
Input

Physical QWERTY Keyboard

A full keyboard sat right beneath the screen, letting people type messages, notes and calendar entries far faster than the stylus-only PDAs that came before it.

  • Backlit keys for typing in low light
  • Dedicated shortcut keys for core apps
  • Five-way navigator for one-handed scrolling
📅
Productivity

Full PDA Functions

Palm's organizer heritage carried straight into the Treo, bundling the tools people relied on to plan a day and keep track of the people in it.

  • Calendar with day, week and month views
  • Contacts, Tasks and Memos synced to a desktop
  • Documents To Go for viewing office files
🌐
Connectivity

Internet Access

The built-in Palm OS browser turned the Treo into one of the first phones people could actually use to check the web, not just a phone that technically supported it.

  • Browsing over GPRS / EDGE data networks
  • Bookmarks synced alongside contacts and calendar
  • Optional Bluetooth for tethered laptop access
📞
Telephony

Mobile Phone

Underneath the organizer software was still a capable GSM phone, with the call quality and battery life people expected from a dedicated handset.

  • Speakerphone and voice-dialing support
  • Caller ID linked directly to the Contacts app
  • SMS and MMS messaging built in
📷
Camera

Built-in Camera

A VGA camera on the back meant people could capture a moment and send it along without reaching for a separate device.

  • 0.3 megapixel sensor, VGA resolution stills
  • Photos attachable directly to email and MMS
  • Simple viewer for browsing saved images
